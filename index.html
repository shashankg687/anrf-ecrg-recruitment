Job Description The selected candidate will work on theoretical and numerical aspects of multipartite quantum cryptography, including threshold quantum distillation, multipartite entanglement cloning machines, and quantum secret sharing protocols.

The work involves quantum circuit and network simulations using Python, QuTiP, Qiskit, and MATLAB/Mathematica, along with security proofs, analytical derivations, and manuscript writing for high-impact scientific publications.
